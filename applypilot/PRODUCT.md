# Product Context

**Name:** ApplyPilot
**Tagline:** AI Job & Internship Automation Platform

## Target Audience
Job seekers, fresh graduates, and career-switchers who want to streamline their application process using AI to match with relevant roles, tailor resumes to ATS (Applicant Tracking Systems) standards, and automatically apply or track applications.

## User Goals
- **Efficiency:** Find and apply to suitable roles with minimal manual data entry.
- **Accuracy:** Ensure resumes bypass ATS filters seamlessly.
- **Clarity:** Maintain a clear, bird's-eye view of all active applications, upcoming interviews, and historical success rates.

## Screen Contexts
- **Landing Page:** Desktop-first, highly readable marketing pages conveying trust, technical competence, and ease of use. No gimmicks, just proof of value.
- **Dashboard / Application Tracker:** Dense data environments meant for quick scanning. Focus on utility, typography contrast, and data integrity over flashy animations.

## Tone & Voice
- **Professional, Direct, Empowering:** Speak confidently. Avoid generic buzzwords ("supercharge", "enterprise-grade"). Be concrete ("Automate 50 applications per week", "Score resumes against ATS rubrics").
